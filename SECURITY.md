# Reporting a security issue

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem in Lanyard.

Email **security@nxsysdev.com** with:

- the Lanyard version and build (Settings → About),
- your macOS version,
- steps to reproduce, and what an attacker could gain.

You will get an acknowledgement within three business days. We ask that you give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before publishing anything about it, and we will credit you in the release notes if you want that.
